Upway Brooklyn Warehouse Entry & Test Ride Waiver
UPWAY USA INC · Upway Brooklyn UpCenter · 140 Morgan Ave, Brooklyn, NY
Customer Acknowledgment & Release of Liability

1. Age verification

By entering the Upway Brooklyn warehouse and test-riding bicycles, you confirm that you are 18 years of age or older — or that you are accompanied by a parent or legal guardian — and that you meet the minimum legal age required to operate the specific bicycle or e-bike you wish to ride.

2. Acknowledgment of risks

You acknowledge that visiting an active warehouse and test-riding bicycles involve significant risks, including but not limited to:

  • Loss of control or falls while riding or handling bicycles
  • Collision with warehouse equipment, fixtures, or infrastructure (shelving, support columns, workbenches, forklifts, loading equipment)
  • Crashes, impacts, or falls resulting in personal injury (cuts, bruises, fractures, head injury, or worse)
  • Property damage to bicycles, warehouse equipment, or merchandise caused by your actions
  • Uneven surfaces, obstacles, or debris on the warehouse floor
  • Limited visibility or lighting in certain areas of the facility
  • Mechanical failure of a bicycle during a test ride (brake failure, drivetrain malfunction, loss of steering control)

You understand that these risks are inherent to the warehouse environment and test-riding activities, and cannot be completely eliminated by UPWAY USA INC's safety measures.

3. Voluntary assumption of risk

You voluntarily assume all risks associated with your visit to the Upway Brooklyn warehouse and your participation in test-riding bicycles. You understand that you are responsible for your own physical safety and well-being during your visit.

4. Release of liability

In consideration for being allowed to enter the warehouse and test-ride bicycles, you hereby release, waive, and discharge UPWAY USA INC, its owners, managers, employees, and agents from any and all claims, demands, causes of action, and liability arising out of or related to:

  • Personal injury (including death) sustained during your warehouse visit or test ride
  • Property damage caused by you or resulting from the test ride
  • Economic losses or medical expenses resulting from the above

This release applies except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct by UPWAY USA INC.

5. Customer liability for damages

You agree that you are fully responsible and liable for any damage to UPWAY USA INC property, bicycles, merchandise, or warehouse equipment caused by your actions, including but not limited to:

  • Crashes or collisions resulting from riding beyond your skill level or ability
  • Damage caused by reckless riding or failure to follow safety instructions
  • Damage to bicycles or equipment caused by improper handling or misuse
  • Property damage resulting from your negligence

You understand that you may be billed for the full cost of repairs or replacement of any damaged property.

6. Agreement to follow safety instructions

You agree to:

  • Follow all safety instructions provided by UPWAY USA INC staff before and during your visit
  • Wear appropriate footwear and clothing for a warehouse environment
  • Obey all posted signs and verbal directions from UPWAY USA INC personnel
  • Immediately stop any activity and notify staff if you feel unsafe or experience a mechanical issue
  • Not operate a bicycle if you are under the influence of drugs or alcohol, or if you lack the physical ability to safely ride

7. Medical emergency consent

You consent to receive emergency medical attention from paramedics or emergency medical personnel if needed during your visit. You authorize UPWAY USA INC staff to contact emergency services (911) on your behalf if you are injured or become ill. You understand that any emergency medical services provided by paramedics or hospitals are at your own expense, and UPWAY USA INC is not responsible for those costs.

8. Acknowledgment

  • You understand the risks involved in warehouse entry and test-riding bicycles
